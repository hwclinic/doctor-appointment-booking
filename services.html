Our Services

Dr. Revathy provides personalised homoeopathic consultations for individuals and families. Each consultation is tailored to the person's concerns, health history, symptoms, and individual circumstances.

General Consultation

A personalised consultation for new health concerns, recurring symptoms, and general wellbeing. The consultation provides an opportunity to discuss your concerns, health history, lifestyle, and individual needs.

Chronic Condition Follow-up

Regular consultations for people living with long-standing or recurring health concerns. Follow-up appointments provide an opportunity to review changes and discuss the next steps in your care.

Child Health

Individualised consultations for children and adolescents, with attention to their age, development, symptoms, health history, and individual circumstances. Parents or legal guardians participate throughout the consultation.

Women's Health

Personalised consultations addressing women's health concerns across different stages of life, with consideration given to symptoms, health history, lifestyle, and individual circumstances.

Counselling & Wellbeing Support

A confidential and supportive space to discuss emotional wellbeing, stress, life changes, and challenges that may be affecting your quality of life.

Online Consultations

All consultations are currently conducted online. After booking, you will receive your appointment reference and, for online consultations, the details needed to join your video consultation.

If your concern requires urgent medical attention, diagnostic investigation, or specialist care, please seek appropriate medical services.
