Consultation areas

Every appointment is a conversation shaped around the person—not a one-size-fits-all answer. Here are the areas you can discuss with Dr. Revathy.

General consultation

Talk through a new concern, recurring symptoms, your health history and what you would like help understanding.

Ongoing follow-up

Review changes since an earlier appointment and discuss questions or next steps in your ongoing care.

Child and adolescent health

Age-aware consultations that take a child’s needs and circumstances into account, with a parent or legal guardian involved.

Women’s health

Discuss women’s health concerns across different stages of life, with attention to your individual history and circumstances.

A supportive conversation

Emotional wellbeing

A supportive space to talk about stress, life changes and emotional wellbeing. For urgent concerns or specialist mental-health care, please contact an appropriate professional service.
