Our Services

Dr. Revathy provides personalised homoeopathic consultations for individuals and families. Each consultation is centred on understanding the person's concerns, symptoms, health history, lifestyle, and individual circumstances.

General Consultation

A personalised consultation for new health concerns, recurring symptoms, and general wellbeing.

The consultation provides an opportunity to discuss your concerns, health history, lifestyle, and individual needs in a comfortable and supportive setting.

Chronic Condition Follow-up

Regular consultations for people seeking continued support with long-standing or recurring health concerns.

Follow-up appointments provide an opportunity to review changes since the previous consultation and discuss the next steps in your care.

Child Health

Individualised consultations for children and adolescents, taking into consideration their age, development, symptoms, health history, and individual circumstances.

Parents or legal guardians participate throughout the consultation.

Women's Health

Personalised consultations addressing women's health concerns across different stages of life.

Consultations consider the individual's symptoms, health history, lifestyle, and circumstances.

Counselling & Wellbeing Support

A confidential and supportive space to discuss stress, emotional wellbeing, life changes, and personal challenges that may be affecting your overall wellbeing.

Where concerns require specialised mental-health or medical care, appropriate professional services should be sought.

Online Consultations

All consultations are currently conducted online. This allows patients to consult with Dr. Revathy remotely from the comfort and privacy of their own home.

Online consultations are available for patients who are able to participate through a suitable internet-connected device.

After booking, you will receive a booking reference. For online consultations, the video consultation details will be provided when available.

If a health concern requires urgent medical attention, diagnostic investigation, or specialist care, please seek appropriate medical services.
