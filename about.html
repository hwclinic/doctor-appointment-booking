About Dr. [Revathy]

About the Doctor Dr. [Revathy], BHMS Dr. [Revathy] is a qualified Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS) practitioner with more than 10 years of experience in homeopathic practice. With a patient-centred approach, she focuses on understanding each individual's health concerns, lifestyle, and overall well-being before developing a personalized approach to care. Her experience in homeopathy has given her the opportunity to work with patients from different age groups and with a variety of health concerns. In addition to her medical qualification, Dr. [Revathy] is also a Certified Yoga Trainer. She believes that good health involves more than addressing individual symptoms and that healthy lifestyle practices, movement, relaxation, and mindfulness can play an important role in overall well-being. Her approach brings together her experience in homeopathy and yoga, with an emphasis on personalized care, healthy living, and helping patients develop sustainable wellness habits. Dr. [Revathy] is committed to providing a welcoming and comfortable environment where patients can discuss their health concerns and receive individual attention.
