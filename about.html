About Dr. Revathy

Dr. Revathy, BHMS

Dr. Revathy is a Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS) graduate with more than 10 years of experience in homoeopathic practice.

With a patient-centred approach, she focuses on understanding each individual's health concerns, symptoms, health history, lifestyle, and individual circumstances before discussing an appropriate approach to care.

Her experience in homoeopathy has given her the opportunity to work with people from different age groups and with a variety of health concerns.

Yoga & Wellbeing

In addition to her BHMS qualification, Dr. Revathy is also a Certified Yoga Trainer. She believes that healthy lifestyle practices, movement, relaxation, and mindfulness can play an important role in supporting overall wellbeing.

Her interests in homoeopathy and yoga come together in an approach that recognises the importance of personalised care, healthy living, and sustainable wellbeing habits.

Community & Volunteer Experience

Dr. Revathy has also contributed her time to community-oriented healthcare initiatives. She volunteered with the Ramakrishna Mission in Bartley, Singapore, where she attended to patients.

She has also been involved in volunteering for a sclerosis-related project, contributing to a community initiative supporting people affected by the condition.

Online Consultations

The clinic currently offers consultations online. This allows patients to consult with Dr. Revathy remotely from the comfort and privacy of their own home.

After booking an online appointment, patients receive their booking reference and the details needed for their video consultation.

A Personal Approach to Care

Dr. Revathy is committed to providing a welcoming and comfortable environment where patients can discuss their health concerns openly and receive individual attention.

Where a health concern requires urgent attention, diagnostic investigation, or specialist medical care, patients should seek appropriate medical services.
