Care begins with understanding

Dr. Revathy takes time to understand each person’s concerns, symptoms, health history, lifestyle and circumstances before discussing an appropriate approach to care.

Her experience has included working with people across age groups and with a variety of health concerns. She has also contributed to community-oriented initiatives, including volunteering with the Ramakrishna Mission in Bartley, Singapore.

Whole-person wellbeing

As a Certified Yoga Trainer, Dr. Revathy is interested in how movement, relaxation and mindful everyday habits can support general wellbeing alongside personalised care.
